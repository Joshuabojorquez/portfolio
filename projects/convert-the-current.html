The Brief

BigBattery's homepage and key landing pages, covering solar, golf cart, and emerging markets, carried the weight of a growing B2B/B2C e-commerce business. The mandate: make every visit measurably more likely to convert.

The Approach

  • Owned UX strategy end to end: research, wireframing, prototyping, sprint planning & KPI measurement
  • Ran conversion-focused redesigns across homepage and landing pages in an agile, data-driven cycle
  • Benchmarked competitive pricing across voltage ranges to sharpen offers and positioning
  • Spearheaded performance work (CDN integration, caching architecture & Core Web Vitals) so speed reinforced the design
