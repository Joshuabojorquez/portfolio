The Brief

BigBattery wanted its own flagship in the lithium home-energy market: a battery for off-grid homeowners who want to be self-sufficient, running on their own solar and their own storage.

I was the only product designer on it, and on every product that followed. The first NEXUS became the base the rest of the line was built on: the inverters that turned it into a complete system, then a second generation.

My Role

  • Form and industrial design: body and panel measurements, tolerances and label placement
  • Color: the Pantone specification for the casing and the branding
  • Concept iteration across multiple rounds of mockups, through executive review to approval
  • Packaging: the retail box for the APEX inverters, designed with AI and CorelDRAW
  • Launch: product animation, website infographics, sale banners and email creative
  • Coordination with the B2B and marketing teams, so product, packaging and launch shipped together

The Render Workflow

The gen 1 launch animation was outsourced to a render artist: thousands of dollars and a two to three week turnaround. For NEXUS 2 I built an AI workflow instead, producing several animated renders of the new product for under $150, with the same impact at launch or better.
