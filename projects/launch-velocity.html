The Brief

High-velocity product launches across BigBattery's solar and golf-cart lines needed sustained demand, not just a spike on launch day. The whole path had to hold together: the ad a buyer first saw, the landing page it sent them to, and the sales pipeline behind it.

The Approach

  • Coordinated each launch with UX, SEO and dev so the landing page, the ads and the video went live together rather than one at a time
  • Translated dense technical specs into short-form product storytelling built for social
  • Produced the launch video and paid ad campaigns that fed each landing page
  • Tracked KPIs post-launch and iterated creative against real performance data
