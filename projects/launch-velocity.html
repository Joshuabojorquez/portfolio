The Brief

High-velocity product launches across BigBattery's solar and golf-cart lines needed sustained demand, not just a spike on launch day. Video had to carry the story from awareness through to the sales pipeline.

The Approach

  • Produced and executed launch video content and paid ad campaigns for each product drop
  • Translated dense technical specs into short-form product storytelling built for social
  • Coordinated launch timing with UX, SEO and dev teams so landing pages, ads and video hit together
  • Tracked KPIs post-launch and iterated creative against real performance data
